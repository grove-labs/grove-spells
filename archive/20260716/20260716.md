## List of intended changes

### Ethereum Mainnet

**Onboard the Paxos USDG bridge (USDC out)**

| rate limit | before max | before slope | after max | after slope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `LIMIT_ASSET_TRANSFER` (USDC -> Paxos) | `0` | `0` | `50_000_000e6` | `50_000_000e6 / 1 days` |


**Transfer all syrupUSDC held by the ALM Proxy to the Spark ALM Proxy**

- Sends the full syrupUSDC balance (~85.9M at time of writing) from the Grove ALM Proxy to the Spark ALM Proxy
- Atomic in-spell transfer (temporary `CONTROLLER` role grant + `ALMProxy.doCall` + revoke), mirroring the Spark September 4, 2025 BUIDL-I transfer to Grove, in reverse

### Robinhood

**Initialize Grove Liquidity Layer**

- Activate the `ForeignController` v1.8.0 via `ForeignControllerInit.initAlmSystem`
- Set the `ForeignController` as `CONTROLLER` on the ALM Proxy and RateLimits
- Grant `RELAYER_ROLE` to both Grove ALM Relayers
- Grant `FREEZER_ROLE` to the Grove Freezer Proxy
