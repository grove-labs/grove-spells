**Set the Grove PAU unwind rate limits to unlimited**

| rate limit           | before max      | before slope             | after max           | after slope |
| ---                  | ---             | ---                      | ---                 | ---         |
| `LIMIT_USDS_BURN`    | `15_000_000e18` | `17_915_904e18 / 1 days` | `type(uint256).max` | `0`         |
| `LIMIT_USDC_TO_USDS` | `15_000_000e6`  | `17_915_904e6 / 1 days`  | `type(uint256).max` | `0`         |

Before values as read on 2026-09-11; PAS steps these keys daily, so both are set unlimited from whatever finite value they hold at execution. The outbound directions (`LIMIT_USDS_MINT`, `LIMIT_USDS_TO_USDC`) keep their finite limits.

### Base

**Onboard the Grove x Steakhouse USDC Morpho Vault** (`GROVE_X_STEAKHOUSE_USDC_V2_MORPHO_VAULT`)

| rate limit            | before max | before slope | after max           | after slope             |
| ---                   | ---        | ---          | ---                 | ---                     |
| `LIMIT_4626_DEPOSIT`  | `0`        | `0`          | `20_000_000e6`      | `20_000_000e6 / 1 days` |
| `LIMIT_4626_WITHDRAW` | `0`        | `0`          | `type(uint256).max` | `0`                     |

| parameter         | before | after     |
| ---               | ---    | ---       |
| max exchange rate | `0`    | `1.15e24` |

Set via `setMaxExchangeRate(vault, 1e18, 1.15e6)`; the stored rate is 1e36-scaled, so `1e36 * 1.15e6 / 1e18` = `1.15e24`. Required before the first deposit, which reverts while the mapping reads `0`.
